> <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide/wrench.svg?color=%2358a6ff" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" /> I take electronics from concept to production — schematic capture, PCB layout, design for manufacturability (DFM), and full PCBA bring-up — with a focus on **high-current power electronics**, **motor control**, and **embedded MCU boards**. On the mechanical side, I design the parts and motion that turn boards into working machines: **sheet metal**, **3D-printed parts**, and **pneumatics**.
